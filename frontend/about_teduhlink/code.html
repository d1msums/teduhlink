About TeduhLink Prototype

Student project · Cyberjaya rider shelter concept

TeduhLink helps riders find nearby shelter during rain and compare estimated crowding before choosing where to stop. Our indoor prototype combines an ESP32 rain-contact sensor with camera-based people counting. Riders can browse shelters at any time.

CONTROLLED INDOOR PROTOTYPE BENCHESP32 + CAM

Actual bench setup: ESP32 microcontroller, MH-RD rain sensor plate, and optical camera module tested in a controlled indoor demonstration area.

sensors

What TeduhLink Actually Measures

Hardware installed directly on physical canopy structures. We explicitly separate raw ground-truth telemetry from inferences.

water_drop Rain Contact
MH-RD Plate

An MH-RD plate detects water contact. For outdoor use, expose the plate to rain. Indoor water application simulates this contact; the plate may remain wet after rain stops.

warning It does NOT measure road friction coefficient, highway corridor rainfall rates, or forecast future rain arrival fronts.
groups Shelter Crowding
Edge Vision

Estimated using a fixed prototype camera running a lightweight pretrained person-detection model. It counts detected people in the shelter zone.

warning It does NOT detect motorcycle parking spaces, identify vehicle models, or guarantee bay reservations for bikes.
cell_tower Data Connectivity
2-5s Cycle

Direct HTTP telemetry upload endpoint updating continuously every 2 to 5 seconds per field node.

What is Simulated or Controlled

To rigorously test edge conditions, latency failures, and UX ergonomics during field runs, some components are synthetic.

Demo Thresholds Fixed Rule

Categories ("No people detected", "Some people detected", "Busy", "Crowding unavailable") are calibrated with an arbitrary demo threshold of 6+ individuals for safety margin benchmarking.

Stop B & C Feeds Synthetic Stream

Simulated telemetry feeds generated to test intermittent LTE network dropouts, high-packet-loss storms, and sudden density fluctuations across peripheral routes.

Navigation Core External Handoff

Turn-by-turn guidance triggers Google Maps or Waze directly. TeduhLink does not execute an autonomous in-app routing or map tile engine.

Rider Safety Charter

"Riders make their own stop decisions. TeduhLink provides grounded local observations to assist your choice, never telling you to race the weather or divert."

  • check Never speed or disregard road conditions to beat a rain indicator.
  • check Shelter stops are public spaces; respect fellow riders and keep egress open.
  • check Always yield to emergency vehicles during high-precipitation flash incidents.
